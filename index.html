About Us 🐾

Growthwell Health Care is a dedicated animal care manufacturing company specializing in high-quality products for dogs and cats. As a proudly local manufacturer, we are committed to improving the health, hygiene, and overall well-being of pets through safe, effective, and affordable solutions.

Who We Are

Growthwell was established with a passion for animal care and a vision to support pet owners with reliable, locally produced products. We understand the importance of maintaining pet health, which is why we focus on creating formulations that meet both quality standards and practical needs.

What We do

We manufacture a wide range of pet care products, including:
Nutritional supplements and vitamins
Medicated and herbal shampoos
Grooming and hygiene products
Specialty care solutions for dogs and cats
Each product is carefully developed to ensure safety, effectiveness, and ease of use.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, locally manufactured pet care products that enhance the health, comfort, and happiness of animals while building trust with pet owners.

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted brand in the pet care industry by delivering innovative, affordable, and reliable products for animal well-being.
